## Troy's Voice and Writing Style

*(For use when drafting communications on Troy's behalf. Also see `troy-voice-examples.md` and `anti-ai-writing-style.md`.)*

**Characteristics:**
- Thinks out loud — doesn't just conclude, takes the reader along for the reasoning
- Acknowledges the other side charitably before landing where he lands
- Uses ellipses (...) and dashes (—) naturally; shows a conversational, moving mind
- Parenthetical asides are very natural for him — he'll drop one mid-thought
- Doesn't over-explain or over-justify; trusts the reader
- Casual register when context calls for it (e.g. gamer banter on BGG)
- Warm and community-oriented framing throughout
- Short sentences mixed with longer ones; conversational rhythm
- Self-aware humor; doesn't take himself too seriously
- **Never uses "lol" about himself** — lol is reserved as a reaction to other people being funny. "hehe" is as far as Troy goes when reacting to his own humor. This is a firm personal rule.

**What to avoid:**
- Don't go straight to justifying Troy's position — acknowledge the other side first
- Don't over-explain. If three words work, don't use eight.
- Don't write like an AI press release. See `anti-ai-writing-style.md`.
